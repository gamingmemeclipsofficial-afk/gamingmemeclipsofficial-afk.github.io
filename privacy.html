Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Clip Gaming is a personal desktop application that runs only on its owner's Windows PC. It is used by a single person, the owner of the Gaming Meme Clips Official channel, to publish clips to their own YouTube and TikTok accounts.

Data the app accesses

  • Google / YouTube (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube): the app uploads the video files the owner approved, together with their title, description and tags; sets or changes their scheduled publish time; reads their upload and privacy status; and reads the name of the owner's own channel.
  • TikTok (scopes user.info.basic, user.info.profile, video.upload): the app reads the owner's display name and username, uploads the approved video to the owner's TikTok inbox as a draft, and reads the status of that upload.

How the data is stored and used

  • Access and refresh tokens are stored only in a local file on the owner's PC. They are never sent anywhere except to Google and TikTok themselves, to make the requests listed above.
  • The app has no servers, no analytics and no advertising. It does not sell, share, transfer or disclose any data to anyone, and it does not use the data for any purpose other than publishing the owner's own clips.
  • Clip Gaming's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube

Clip Gaming uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy applies to the data Google processes.

Removing access and data

  • Press «Scollega» (disconnect) in the app: it deletes the stored tokens from the PC.
  • Google access can also be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • TikTok access can be removed in the TikTok app: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions.
