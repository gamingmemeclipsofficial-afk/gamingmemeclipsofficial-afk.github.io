Clip Gaming

Clip Gaming is a personal desktop tool used by the creator of the Gaming Meme Clips Official channel. It runs only on the creator's own Windows PC and helps prepare short vertical gaming clips (subtitles, titles, hashtags) and publish them to the creator's own YouTube and TikTok accounts at scheduled times.

It is not a public service: there are no user accounts, no servers and no data shared with anyone. The only person who signs in with Google or TikTok is the channel owner.

What it does with YouTube and TikTok

  • YouTube (YouTube Data API): uploads the clips the owner approved, with their title, description and tags, sets the scheduled publish time, and reads the upload status and the channel name.
  • TikTok (Login Kit and Content Posting API, upload as draft): sends the approved clip to the owner's TikTok inbox as a draft, reads the owner's display name and the status of the upload. The owner finishes and posts it in the TikTok app.
